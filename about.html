The short version

Most ISO 27001 consultancies are founded by auditors who learned to sell. Riskfold is the other way round — a commercial background in retail, e-commerce, and customer success at a GRC platform, paired with a lead implementer certification, built specifically to work with founders and commercial teams rather than only with security departments.

That matters in practice: the questionnaire on your desk isn't really a security problem, it's a sales problem wearing a security costume. Riskfold treats it that way — the goal is the deal moving forward, and certification is how it gets there.

Delivery on implementations is led by experienced delivery partners, each screened for real certification track record before being named to a client. Riskfold owns the relationship and the outcome throughout.
