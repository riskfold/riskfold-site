Approach

One owner, start to certification

You get a single point of contact for the whole engagement. Here's what actually happens at each stage, and who's doing the work.

01

Scoping call

Thirty minutes. What's driving the need — a specific deal, an investor requirement, general risk reduction — and which offer actually fits. Most calls end with a clear next step, not a proposal to read later.

02

Gap assessment (if needed)

A structured look at your current state against ISO 27001's requirements. You get an honest picture of the distance to certification before committing to it — including cases where the honest answer is "not yet."

03

Implementation

Risk assessment, control design, and documentation, led by a delivery partner with real certification experience, project-managed by Riskfold. Scope decisions — what's genuinely in and out of the ISMS — get made deliberately, not defaulted to "everything," which is the single most common reason implementations overrun.

04

Internal audit

An independent reviewer — not the person who implemented your controls — checks the work before it goes anywhere near a certification body. Independence is structural, not a formality.

05

Certification audit

Booked and paid directly with an accredited certification body (Nemko, DNV, Kiwa, or BSI). Riskfold prepares you for it and attends — the relationship with the body is yours, not a black box you're routed through.

06

Maintenance

Certification is a starting line, not a finish line. Surveillance audits happen annually — the retainer keeps the ISMS current so recertification never becomes a scramble.

Who actually does the work. Riskfold owns the client relationship, the project timeline, and every commercial decision. Technical delivery — risk methodology, control design, audit prep — is led by an experienced delivery partner, reviewed at every stage. You're told exactly who's doing what before the engagement starts. As Riskfold takes on more implementations directly, that split shifts — but it's never hidden either way.
